# Radio Frequency, SDR & Antenna Engineering

> [!abstract] Spectrum Analysis & Physical Layer Wireless Engineering
> Exploring the electromagnetic spectrum through Software Defined Radio (SDR), automated ADS-B flight tracking, packet decoding, and custom antenna design.

---

### ◈ Systems & Methodologies

1. **Software Defined Radio (SDR) Interception:**
   * RTL-SDR v4, HackRF One, and LimeSDR deployments for wideband signal demodulation (100 kHz - 6 GHz).
   * Decoding unencrypted digital telemetry streams (APRS, AIS, P25, POCSAG, Inmarsat).

2. **ADS-B Flight Tracking Telemetry:**
   * High-gain 1090 MHz and 978 MHz colinear coaxial antennas feed automated dump1090/readsb receiver nodes.
   * Streaming real-time Mode-S aircraft positional vectors to local mapping engines and flight aggregator networks.

3. **Antenna Theory & Construction:**
   * Custom fabrication and tuning of resonant dipole, quarter-wave ground plane, and V-dipole antennas for NOAA weather satellite APT signal capture.
