> [!tip] Relocated to RPDev Blog & Research

## Architectural Problem Statement

Security and AI systems engineering spans infrastructure architecture, agent authorization, incident response, regulatory compliance, telemetry, and edge deployment. Treating these areas as isolated articles obscures how shared foundations—cryptographic identity, data handling, network boundaries, evidence quality, and operational ownership—govern the reliability and safety of an enterprise environment.

This specification index serves as an executive research gateway, orienting engineering leaders to topic-specific architecture monographs and connecting each pattern to verifiable threat models, regulatory controls (`NIST CSF 2.0`, `ISO 27001`, `SOC 2`), and production operational requirements.

Readers should follow the deep-link references to each monograph whenever full implementation blueprints, configuration schemas, or empirical benchmark evidence are needed to evaluate a design decision.

## Core Design & Implementation

- Organize applied research by durable engineering domains and preserve stable routes and aliases so cross-repository documentation and policy references remain verifiable as the corpus evolves.
- Connect interdependent control planes explicitly: bind agent execution to cryptographic identity and tool authorization, tie telemetry to automated incident response, and anchor architectural claims to governance policies and CMDB baselines.
- Separate conceptual reference patterns from lab-validated implementations, document boundary assumptions clearly, and direct engineers to primary configuration manifests and post-mortem records in the **[[Security-and-AI-Research/Codex_Arcana|Engineering Post-Mortems & ADR Vault]]**.
- Maintain the specification as a versioned knowledge system with periodic drift reviews, deprecation tracking, and deterministic fallback navigation for external citations.

## Enterprise Operational Takeaway

Use this specification index to locate relevant architectural patterns, then validate their security controls and failure modes against your production environment's compliance and reliability baselines.
