> [!abstract] Corporate Governance & Compliance
> Technical controls are only as effective as the policies that enforce them. This domain covers my experience authoring, enforcing, and defending enterprise-wide Information Security policies during rigorous third-party audits.

Moving beyond technical implementation, I have successfully navigated exhaustive information security audits for multi-national organizations, transforming raw compliance requirements into actionable, practical workflows for engineering and HR teams.

---

### ◈ Authored Policies & Frameworks

I specialize in writing clear, unambiguous corporate IT policies that align with industry best practices (e.g., NIST, CIS Controls) without paralyzing daily business operations.

- **Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC):** Authored strict security enforcement guidelines for internal development teams, mandating code reviews, static analysis, and vulnerability scanning before production deployments.
- **Vendor Risk Management (VRM):** Established protocols for evaluating the security posture of third-party cloud services, integrating MSA (Master Service Agreement) negotiations with technical security reviews.
- **Acceptable Use & Physical Security:** Defined and enforced policies restricting removable media (USB drives), standardizing physical access control protocols, and mandating clean-desk environments for compliance-heavy departments.

### ◈ Third-Party Security Audits

> [!success] Audit Defense & Remediation

### ◈ Continuous Compliance (GRC)

Modern governance requires continuous monitoring, not just annual checklists.
- **CISO Assistant Integration:** Deployed and managed GRC (Governance, Risk, and Compliance) platforms like CISO Assistant to continuously track organizational compliance against 150+ open-source and federal frameworks.
- **Automated Evidence Collection:** Engineered telemetry pipelines to automatically aggregate logs and system states, significantly reducing the administrative overhead required during the evidence-collection phase of an audit.
