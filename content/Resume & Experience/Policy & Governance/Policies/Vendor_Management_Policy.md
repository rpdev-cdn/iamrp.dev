Vendor Management Policy
                                          



Approval Date                September 4, 2012

Approved By                  Andrew A. Paisley

Purpose




It is the policy of the Firm to retain outside counsel and vendors who are competent, reliable, financially
sound, terminable at-will and who can maintain confidences per our client requirements. Prior to
entering into any engagement with Vendors who may have access or who could gain access to our
facilities or to confidential client information such as, but not limited to, non-public private information,
the Firm shall consider and review the vendor’s security to the extent applicable, review the company’s
financial health, perform a thorough conflict of interest, review the vendor’s policies and procedures,
check references of the vendor and ensure that its employees are screened with appropriate background
checks.

Outside Counsel and Vendors must furnish copies of certificates of insurance evidencing required
coverage. Outside Counsel and Vendors, including affiliated companies, will be required to
acknowledge these requirements in writing. Before engaging any vendor (including affiliated
companies) for which our client will be charged, the Firm will conduct a price comparison and
document same. In order to determine whether the vendor’s pricing is competitive, market surveys of
recoverable third party vendors costs will be conducted on an annual basis and the results of such
surveys will be retained by the Compliance Officer and made available to a client upon request. Clients
will be notified via the preferred method of communication prior to adding any new vendor to our third
party vendor matrix.

Selection Criteria
      Performance history
      Completion of annual training
      Copy of SLA (to include security and privacy criteria)
      BCP/DR plan and test results for high-risk vendors (retained for a period of 7 years or as long as
       the firm retains the vendor, whichever is longer)
      Written disclosure from vendor if any work will be performed offshore. If so, the vendor must
       provide a detailed list of tasks that will be performed offshore.

Performance Requirements
Scorecards for title companies and process serving companies are run from our case management system
on a monthly basis.

                        Vendor Management Policy
                                        

Contingency Protocol
If a loss of relationship or operational process occurs, we have identified other vendors who would be
available and would meet the needs of our clients. All potential replacement vendors will be vetted in
accordance with the procedure set forth above.

Vendor Off-Boarding
In the event that a relationship with an existing vendor is terminated, the off-boarding procedures
detailed below should be followed to both protect client data and properly and legally wind down the
relationship with the party:
      Review the contract and any amendments, if applicable, for terms associated with the
       termination of the agreement
      Advise point of contact about intention to terminate; make inquiries in regard to secure erasure
       of data
      If applicable, ramp up operations with new vendor until such time as operations with terminated
       vendor can be permanently suspended
      Remove any physical and/or information security access including premises access and Active
       Directory and database accounts
      Securely erase data stored with the vendor; if this is a process that must be performed by the
       vendor, consider having the vendor sign a statement to that effect (that data will be securely
       erased)
      Inform appropriate parties within The JDC Family of Companies that the vendor is being
       deprecated / off-boarded
      Archive all data about the use of the vendor including contracts, amendments, special
       agreements, procedures etc.
   Upon expiration or termination of outside counsel or a vendor, law firm will formally notify outside
   counsel or vendor of termination in writing, promptly request return to law firm of such information
   that was received and destroy all documentation in any medium that contains, refers to or relates to
   confidential information, notify the appropriate law firm personnel and/or department of the
   expiration or termination of outside counsel or vendor relationship and law firm will immediately
   notify client of the change in vendor status.
