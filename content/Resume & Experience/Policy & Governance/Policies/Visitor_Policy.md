Visitor Policy
                                          



Approval Date                6/3/2011

Approved By                  Andrew A. Paisley

Purpose




For the safety and security of our visitors and employees,  (CH),
requires that all visitors to the building use our main entrance which is located on the
Southeast side of the building directly behind the JDC Family of Company’s sign. All
visitors and contractors must park in the front parking area. The main entrance will be the
only point to enter and exit the building during normal business hours. All other doors
remain locked and are used only for entering or exiting with an employee issued key fob
and emergency exit purposes.

Upon arrival, the receptionist shall verify the identity of the visitor by reviewing a driver’s
license or other similar government issued photo identification, inquire as to whom the
visitor is meeting and notify the host employee. All visitors shall sign the visitors log
book at the reception desk and be issued a visitor badge which is to be worn at all times
while the person is on the premises. The visitor badge shall set forth the name, company
and date of the visit and contain an expiration mark that appears after 24 hours after it has
been issued. Visitors include any person not an employee of the firm. The receptionist
shall advise the person who is expecting the visitor of the visitor’s arrival and that person
must accompany the visitor(s) for the duration of their stay. In addition to issuing and
retrieving visitor badges, the receptionist is responsible for ensuring that the visitor sign-in
into the visitor log the date and time of the arrival, the person being visited and the date
and time of the departure.

Vendors and service personnel delivering items to the building do not need a visitors
badge or escort unless that person is leaving the reception area.

Any employee who notices a visitor without a badge should bring the situation to the
attention of a member of leadership immediately.

Employees who have scheduled meetings with a visitor to the building before or after
normal business hours (8:30a.m.- 5:00 p.m. M-F) must make arrangements to meet them
at the Southeast entrance. Compliance with the sign in/sign out and visitor badge policies
is the responsibility of the employee host.



                                                 
                                      Visitor Policy
                                         

Employees are not permitted to allow anyone to enter the building. Employees are to
direct visitors to the receptionist. Visitors who appear at the office outside of normal
business hours should be informed they are not permitted into the building until the person
with whom the appointment is made appears at the Southeast entrance to escort them. As
a courtesy to our visitors, the employee entering the building should advise the employee
host of the visitor. Any employee who anticipates a visitor, should notify the front desk in
advance by email indicating who is coming, when they are arriving and how many are in
the visiting party.

All employees are responsible for the security of our building. “Tailgating” refers to the
act of an unauthorized person following an employee into the building without the consent
of the employee. If an employee is entering the building using a key fob and a stranger
attempts to follow the employee into the building while the door is open, the employee
must ask the person to identify himself and state what business the person has. If the
person has legitimate business with someone on the property, then they must escort the
employee to the front desk to comply with building security policies. If the tailgating
occurs after hours, the employee is to direct the stranger to make an appointment during
regular business hours and return later. If the person has an appointment and it is after
hours, the person is to ascertain who the appointment is with, ask the stranger to wait
outside and then get the employee with whom the stranger has the appointment.

The Compliance Officer shall be responsible for reminding all employees by email on a
quarterly basis about everyone’s responsibility for building security as delineated in this
policy.

Employees who have forgotten his or her key fob must enter by the Southeast entrance
doors.
