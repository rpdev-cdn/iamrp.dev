  GENERAL BUSINESS CONTINUITY / DISASTER RECOVERY PLAN

Purpose        To set forth a three phase recovery plan in the event of a catastrophic disaster to
               our building.

Objective      To return to business as usual as quickly as possible after a disaster.
               To satisfy our ethical obligations and minimizing risks to our clients and our Firm
               while managing our disaster plan.
               To maintain the continued viability of the Firm after a disaster.

Scope          This plan sets forth all aspects of the recovery plan except IT, accounting, human
               resources and crisis management which are dealt with elsewhere.

Assumption     Our building is unusable for an extended period of time because of a disaster.
               The disaster is localized i.e. electricity, internet and telephone are generally
               available in the region (Northeast Ohio)
               We can access our case management system, accounting software, and phones
               within 24 hours or less after the disaster.
               Our employees generally have access to cell phones, high speed internet and
               home computers and we can communicate with them.
               Our employees are available to work after the disaster.
               Temporary Office space will be available.
               Our building will be rebuilt.
               The vaulting and recovery sites have sufficient geographic diversity and are
               available based upon the risk analysis found in the appendix.

Strategies Used        Employees work from trailers provided by Agility Recovery until we are
                       able to move into temporary office space. Weekly hearing and sales
                       calendars will be emailed to leadership at their homes for easy retrieval in
                       the event of a disaster. Temporary space is obtained while the building is
                       being rebuilt. The building is rebuilt.

Activation Criteria    The “Main Office”/”Main Data Center” or “Branch Office” has been
                       destroyed or otherwise rendered unusable due to natural or an unnatural
                       disaster.

                              implemented.

Recovery Locations Overview
This report lists the physical locations that the plan may use during its execution
(including alternatives) and each site’s role in support of the plan and teams.


Location – “Main Office”/”Main Data Center”

Phase 1: Upon declaration of a disaster, Agility Recovery, a third party disaster recovery vendor
which supplies trailers, computers, printers, desks and other office materials will provide us with
temporary trailers to be located in our parking lot within 48 hours of the disaster declaration. In
the event that Agility is delayed in its implementation of the trailers, the location of work during
the early days after the disaster (up to two weeks) will be at employees’ homes. Command and
control and print/mail distribution will be carried out from these trailers with the personal
residence of the managing partner serving as a backup.

Phase 2: In case of a power outage, the firm has a generator which uses natural gas via a direct
feed pipeline. This pipeline provides a continuous source of fuel as long as the fuel supply is not
interrupted at the source or pipeline. In the event the natural gas generator were to mechanically
fail or lose fuel, a third party disaster recovery vendor, Agility Recovery, provides a generator
onsite to be hard-wired into our mail panels within 48 hours. Temporary work trailers will be
provided by Agility Recovery. If possible, the trailers will be set up in the rear parking lot at the
“Main Office”. Internet and phones will be provided by Agility Recovery. Electric power is
provided by Hudson Power as opposed to AEP which supplies the current Stow, Oh location so
it is unlikely that both areas would be suffering from the same power outages. The following
location will be available in the event of disaster for all core components, work area recovery,
command and control, non-data storage, and print/mail distribution.

Alternate Location

In the event that the trailers cannot be set up in the rear parking lot, then an alternative temporary
site will be chosen as close to the “Main Office”/”Main Data Center” as possible.

Phase 3: Return to “Main Office”.


Location – “Branch Office”

Phase 1: In the event the Kentucky office is struck by disaster, the location of work during the
early days after the disaster (up to two weeks) will be at employees’ homes. Command and
control and print/mail distribution will be carried out at the “Main Office”.

Phase 2: Return to “Branch Office”

                                         Communications


The purpose of this part of the plan is to set forth objectives and strategies for handling
communications with clients, employees and the media.

Our strategy in all communications is to:
       Avoid adverse publicity
       Take advantage of opportunities for useful publicity
       Have answers to the following questions:
       What happened?
       How did it happen?
       What is the Firm doing about it?

Clients must be assured of the existence of the disaster recovery plan. The Managing Partner and
the Director of Policy and Compliance will be primarily responsible for communicating with
clients. The following must be emphasized as soon as facts are confirmed:
         We have a disaster recovery plan in place and it is being implemented;
         All of our data and computer applications have been stored in a fail over site, and will be
         available within 24 hours or less;
         We have accounted for all hearings and sheriff’s sales;
         We are working on returning to business as usual as soon as possible;
         Updates will be provided as they are available.


Employees must be assured that we have a disaster recovery plan in place and all data and
applications will be available for use in 24 hours or less. The following must be emphasized:
       All employees will be advised by their team leaders of their duties and responsibilities as
       soon as possible.
       Advise all employees that our recovery is expected to be in 3 phases: phase 1: work from
       home (remote access to email, client websites and ED); phase 2: work from temporary
       location; phase 3 return to Stow location.
       Our goal is to return to business as usual as soon as possible.

Media/local government will be dealt with by the managing partner.
