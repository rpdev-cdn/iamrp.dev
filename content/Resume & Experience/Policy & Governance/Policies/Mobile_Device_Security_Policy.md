Mobile Device Security Policy
                                    The JDC Family of Companies



Approval Date              06/06/2012

Approved By                Andrew Paisley

                           To briefly describe security expectations to all users accessing
Purpose
                           network resources via mobile devices, primarily smartphones and
                           laptop computers


        All employees of The JDC Family of Companies (“JDC”), who use a mobile computing device
that synchronizes information with JDC resources, are responsible for securing their device to prevent
sensitive data from being lost or compromised, viruses being spread, and other forms of abuse of JDC
computing infrastructure. If a mobile computing device is lost, stolen, or believed to be compromised,
the incident shall be reported according to the “Computer/Mobile Device Incident Policy”.

       The following guidelines ensure that the employees of JDC have a clear understanding of proper
procedure and usage. JDC reserves the right to modify this guideline to address any concerns that can
potentially cause a disruption of services to the institution.

                                        Personal Firewall Requirement

        Mobile devices deployed by the IT Department shall have Trend OfficeScan IPS Firewall
(“OfficeScan”) installed. Additionally, the user will be prevented via OfficeScan Policy from turning
off the firewall. Use of non-company owned mobile computing devices to access JDC resources is
discouraged; the IT Manager has the authority to grant access on a case by case basis. Employees that
are granted rights to use a non-company device to sync with JDC resources should be aware that the IT
Manager or his designee reserves the right to wipe all data from the device (including personal files)
while that link is maintained. The IT Manager or his designee also reserves the right to remotely wipe a
company-owned device. Mobile computing devices that are not capable of running a firewall without
“rooting” the device are not subject to this requirement.

                                            Data encrypted at rest

        Mobile computing devices used to access JDC network resources are required to have all storage
employed by the device, whether removable or not, encrypted at rest. For laptop computers, generally
this encryption shall be provided by a product called “Bitlocker”. The IT Manager or his designee shall
maintain the encryption keys for Bitlocker. For smartphones, this encryption shall be forced upon the
phone using an ActiveSync policy.

                                Use of Device Passcode – Smartphones ONLY
        To ensure that data accessed through your smartphone is secure, especially in the event that it is
misplaced, stolen or believed to be compromised, all smartphones synchronizing with JDC resources are
subject to the following:

      A “device passcode” consisting of at least 6 characters of which 2 must be symbols such as:
       !@#$%^&*().
      The time without user input for the passcode to be re-entered is 30 minutes.
      Passcodes expire after 90 days.
      A passcode history of 10 passcodes shall be enforced.
      After 10 failed attempts to enter the passcode, the device shall automatically be wiped.

NOTE: Mobile Device users who connect to JDC network resources will have the aforementioned
settings automatically enabled on their device through Active Sync.

Please note: It is prohibited to store Bank of America confidential and proprietary data on
laptops and other mobile devices.
