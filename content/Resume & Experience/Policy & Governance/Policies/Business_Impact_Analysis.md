Business Impact Analysis
                          Performed on September 13, 2019

       The purpose of this document is to identify all critical business processes of Clunk,

Hoose Co., LPA and the effect of the loss of these processes in the event of a Business

Continuity Incident.

       First, all key business processes are identified by management. Once these processes

have been identified, each process is given an “owner”—the chief internal party that oversees

that process. From there, each process is examined to determine a recovery time objective

(RTO), a recovery point objective (RPO), and how the loss of the process could potentially

impact the following:      Legal, Regulatory, and Contract; Customer Service; Operations;

Organization Image and Reputation; Leadership and Management Decision Making; and

Financial. Finally, an Alpha rating is applied to the risk associated with the loss of the business

process based on the following scale in order of severity: High; Moderate; Low; None.

       This document has a direct effect on the structure of the Business Continuity and Disaster

Recovery Plan which may adjust dependent upon this analysis.
