# Modern Security Awareness Curriculum

The landscape of cyber threats has evolved beyond traditional email phishing and rudimentary malware. In the modern era, propelled by generative AI and advanced automation, threat actors are leveraging highly sophisticated techniques to compromise the human element. A point-in-time, compliance-driven video training approach is no longer sufficient. 

As a forward-leaning IT and Security leader, I champion a **continuous, behavioral-driven security curriculum** designed to build a resilient, deeply suspicious, yet highly productive workforce.

## Core Curriculum Focus Areas

### 1. AI-Generated Deep-Fakes and Synthetic Media
The proliferation of deep-fake technology has introduced a new vector for executive impersonation and business email compromise (BEC).
*   **Synthetic Audio & Video Recognition:** Training employees to identify anomalies in cadence, tone, and visual artifacts during video conferencing or voice calls.
*   **Out-of-Band Verification Protocols:** Establishing strict, non-repudiable verification workflows for financial transactions or sensitive data requests, regardless of the perceived authority of the requester.

### 2. Advanced Social Engineering
Threat actors are utilizing LLMs to craft hyper-personalized, context-aware attacks at scale.
*   **Beyond the Nigerian Prince:** Educating users on targeted spear-phishing, smishing (SMS phishing), and vishing (voice phishing) campaigns that leverage scraped social media and breached data.
*   **Contextual Suspicion:** Fostering a culture where unsolicited requests for credentials, access, or data are met with immediate verification, irrespective of the medium.

### 3. Zero Trust Principles in Daily Workflows
Zero Trust is not just a network architecture; it is an operational mindset.
*   **Assume Breach Mentality:** Training users to understand that internal networks are inherently hostile and that trust must be continuously verified.
*   **MFA Fatigue & Token Theft:** Educating the workforce on the risks of MFA prompt bombing and the importance of hardware-backed FIDO2 keys and biometric verification over traditional SMS or push notifications.

### 4. Continuous Behavioral Testing
Annual compliance videos fail to produce lasting behavioral change. Our approach relies on continuous, randomized, and tailored testing.
*   **Iterative Simulations:** Deploying regular, varying degrees of simulated phishing and social engineering attacks to gauge departmental and individual resilience.
*   **Just-in-Time Coaching:** Providing immediate, constructive feedback and targeted micro-learning modules when an employee falls for a simulation, rather than punitive measures.
*   **Gamification and Metrics:** Utilizing positive reinforcement and internal leaderboards to encourage reporting of suspicious activity, transforming the workforce into an active sensor network for the SOC.

By pivoting from a check-the-box compliance exercise to an active, intelligence-driven training program, we harden the organization's perimeter where it matters most: the end-user.
