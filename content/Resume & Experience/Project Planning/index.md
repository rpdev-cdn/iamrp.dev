# Project Planning & Architecture

> [!abstract] Technical Roadmapping & Strategic Execution
> Leading multi-million-dollar infrastructure modernizations, data center migrations, disaster recovery failover simulations, and regulatory compliance audits.

---

### ◈ Strategic Domains

1. **Datacenter Migrations & Hybrid Cloud Strategy:**
   * Planning zero-downtime migrations from legacy on-premises metal to hybrid cloud and containerized microservices.
   * Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) optimization and vendor consolidation.

2. **Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity (DR/BC):**
   * Establishing aggressive Recovery Time Objectives (RTO < 4 hrs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO < 1 hr).
   * Executing annual live failover drills between primary datacenters and secondary disaster recovery sites.

3. **Security Architecture & Zero Trust Roadmaps:**
   * Developing phased multi-year roadmaps transitioning enterprise perimeters to identity-centric Zero Trust architectures (NIST 800-207).
