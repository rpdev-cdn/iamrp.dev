* **Federal VHA Turnaround:** Drove the second-largest VA medical center nationwide (Louis Stokes Cleveland VA Medical Center) from **15% to 98%+ compliance** using `ServiceNow`, `Nessus`, and `SCCM`.
* **Multi-Site Federal Handoff:** Directed patching and configuration compliance across 5 Ohio VHA medical centers (`Cleveland`, `Columbus`, `Cincinnati`, `Chillicothe`, `Dayton`) and **6,000+ endpoints**, achieving a **95%+ regional handoff**.
* **Enterprise KACE & WSUS Administration:** Managed `Quest/Dell KACE` and `WSUS` across **70+ servers and 150+ workstations** (`220+ nodes`) for automated CMDB inventory, software licensing, and patch compliance.
* **ITIL Change & Incident Workflows:** Administered `FogBugz` change management boards and Help Desk triage workflows, maintaining **sub-1-hour resolution times (<1h SLA)** on critical production tickets.
* **Automated Drift & CMDB Engine:** Built a `Python 3`, `AsyncSSH`, `Pydantic`, and `ruamel.yaml` audit engine (`Infra Audit Engine`) to baseline multi-node configurations, track hardware and container assets, and detect configuration drift.
* **Directory & GPO Standardization:** Remediated **40+ legacy Active Directory and DNS misconfigurations** and executed structured `Group Policy (GPO)` mapping and cleanup across multi-branch domains.

### 2. Enterprise Security, Compliance & Audit Defense
* **Tier-1 Bank & Agency Audits:** Maintained a **100% audit pass rate** across `Bank of America`, `Capital One`, `Fifth Third Bank`, `HSBC`, `NYCB`, `Fannie Mae`, and `Freddie Mac` institutional security assessments.
* **Multi-Framework Governance:** Led third-party `SOC 2`, `ISO/IEC 27001:2022`, `ISO 27002`, `ALTA Best Practices`, `GLBA`, and quarterly `PCI-DSS` ASV compliance audits alongside `Dell SecureWorks` external penetration tests.
* **Corporate Policy Authorship:** Authored and enforced **20+ corporate security, encryption, change management, acceptable use, and remote-operations policies** plus formal Business Impact Analyses (BIAs).
* **SIEM & Insider-Threat Forensics:** Administered `McAfee SIEM`, `Wazuh`, `CrowdSec`, and custom `PowerShell` log-parsing pipelines to normalize proprietary application telemetry and investigate insider-threat incidents.
* **Endpoint & Physical Hardening:** Enforced `BitLocker` full-disk encryption, `Trend Micro OfficeScan IPS`, `FIDO2` hardware tokens, biometric/keycard access controls, and facility CCTV surveillance.

### 3. Disaster Recovery, Business Continuity & High Availability
* **Global Manufacturing Uptime:** Maintained **99.999% availability** across dense blade enclosures, discrete rack servers, `VMware vSphere` clusters, and `EMC VNX SAN` fabrics at Rockwell Automation's Industrial Data Center.
* **Direct-Hire Selection:** Selected by Rockwell Automation IT leadership **within 60 days of contract start** for conversion to direct full-time W-2 employment.
* **Cross-Hypervisor DR Automation:** Engineered custom `PowerShell` failover orchestration between primary `VMware ESXi` and secondary `Microsoft Hyper-V` clusters, achieving a **sub-30-minute RTO and 15-minute RPO** with zero data loss.
* **Multi-Site Legal Continuity:** Deployed multi-site `Veeam` backup replication with a **sub-15-minute RPO** alongside offsite disaster recovery via `SunGard Availability Services`, `Microsoft Azure`, and `Amazon S3`.
* **Multinational Manufacturing Backups:** Architected `Unitrends` backup appliances and cross-site replication protecting **50+ TB** of engineering and manufacturing data across 5 geographic sites.

### 4. Cloud, Virtualization, Storage & Network Engineering
* **Datacenter & M365 Migration:** Executed an end-to-end onsite-to-hosted datacenter migration and seamless hybrid Exchange-to-`Microsoft 365` deployment for **100+ legal and title users**.
* **High-Availability Perimeter Defense:** Deployed `WatchGuard XTM` high-availability stacked failover firewall clusters, `SonicWall` corporate hardening baselines, `Microsoft UAG`, and `Mimecast` email security.
* **Layer 2 Port Security & VLANs:** Managed core `HP ProCurve` switching with MAC limiting, sticky learning, and `802.1Q` VLAN isolation across **150+ workstations**.
* **Decoupled Container Networking:** Engineered non-NAT Layer 2 container topologies using `macvlan` and `ipvlan` with strict IP Address Management (IPAM) and automated DNS delegation.
* **Spatial Storage Tiering:** Administered `Dell EqualLogic SAN`, `EMC VNX SAN`, `Synology SAN`, and `QNAP NAS` fabrics, and mapped high-I/O `PostgreSQL` databases to NVMe SSDs and honeypots to volatile `tmpfs` RAM disks.

* **Enterprise Scripting & Deployment:** Authored `PowerShell`, `Python 3`, `Bash`, `Batch`, and `WSH/VBScript` automation for Active Directory provisioning, `SCCM` task sequences, and mass endpoint software rollouts.
